Visitor Guidelines & Accessibility

Essential information to ensure an inspiring and seamless journey through the city's foremost historical sanctuary.

Opening Hours

  • Tuesday – Sunday 09:30 – 18:00
  • Thursday Late Nights Until 21:00
  • Mondays Closed for Restoration

Location & Transit

14 Grand Sanctuary Way, Cultural District, City Central.
Direct access via Metropolitan Line (Sanctuary Station) or Tram Line 4. Valet bicycle parking and underground EV charging available.

Accessibility & Etiquette

Full ADA wheelchair accessibility across all 8 gallery wings with complimentary motorized chairs available at information desk. Non-flash photography permitted; tripods restricted.
